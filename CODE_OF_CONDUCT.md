# Community Code of Conduct

Our community values constructive discussion, evidence-based inquiry, and respect across languages, disciplines, and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors with courtesy, even when challenging claims.
- Critique evidence, methods, and arguments rather than people.
- Acknowledge corrections and clearly distinguish uncertainty from established evidence.
- Respect contributors' privacy, cultural context, and intellectual property.
- Keep exchanges focused, factual, and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, doxxing, spam, deliberately disruptive conduct, or persistent personal abuse are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For concerns about community conduct, use the project's [contact page](https://loreandmatter.com/en/contact/) and describe the issue privately. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove inappropriate contributions, and restrict access where appropriate. No specific response time is promised.
