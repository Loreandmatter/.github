# Editorial Principles

Lore & Matter aims to connect historical context with accurate, practical, and appropriately cautious explanations. The site's [editorial standards](https://loreandmatter.com/en/editorial/) provide the public reference.

## Evidence and attribution

- Trace material claims to sources suited to the question: archival documents, standards, technical literature, institutional guidance, and relevant research.
- Distinguish verified information from interpretation, folklore, speculation, and modern reconstruction.
- An earliest surviving reference does **not**, by itself, establish the inventor, place of invention, or date of origin.
- Explain uncertainty, conflicting evidence, and limits of applicability when those details affect conclusions.

## Practical accuracy and safety

- Identify relevant materials, units, test conditions, and environmental constraints.
- Treat health, food preservation, fire, electricity, structures, water, chemicals, and tools as safety-sensitive areas.
- Do not turn experimental findings into universal promises.
- Distinguish educational demonstrations from techniques requiring trained professionals, permits, or regulated standards.

## Translation and accessibility

Articles are available in **Serbian (sr), English (en), German (de), Spanish (es), French (fr), and Portuguese (pt)**. Substantive claims and warnings should remain consistent, while terminology and phrasing can be idiomatic to each language.

Prefer understandable language, informative headings, accessible media descriptions, and precise source attribution.

## AI assistance and corrections

AI can assist with organizing research, drafting, and translation, but an AI output is **not** a factual source or independent expert review. Important claims require verification.

Meaningful content updates and corrections should be transparent. Publication and modification dates should not be artificially advanced by routine builds or purely cosmetic edits.

Read the public [AI-use disclosure](https://loreandmatter.com/en/ai-use/) and [corrections policy](https://loreandmatter.com/en/corrections/).
