# Contributing to Lore & Matter

Lore & Matter welcomes verifiable corrections and responsible collaboration. You do not need to be a software developer to help improve the archive.

## How you can help

- **Factual corrections:** identify the article URL, language, exact passage, and proposed correction.
- **Source suggestions:** provide a reliable primary source, institutional reference, standard, or suitable study, and explain what it supports.
- **Translations:** flag terminology, lost nuance, inconsistent claims, and missing safety warnings.
- **Accessibility:** report unclear headings, non-descriptive image descriptions, difficult navigation, or usability issues.
- **Technical issues:** describe reproducible user-facing problems without including confidential data.

## Where to submit feedback

Read the [public corrections policy](https://loreandmatter.com/en/corrections/) and use the [contact page](https://loreandmatter.com/en/contact/) for article corrections or editorial feedback.

If a particular project repository is public and explicitly accepts issues or pull requests, follow its own CONTRIBUTING instructions. A public GitHub organization profile does **not** mean the entire website source code or article collection is open for contributions.

## Standards for factual edits

1. Quote the exact claim to be revised and distinguish fact from interpretation.
2. Provide independently inspectable supporting sources. AI-generated answers are not primary evidence.
3. Keep historical dates, origins, and attributions precise; the earliest documented record is not automatically the invention date.
4. Preserve relevant measurements, conditions, uncertainty, counterexamples, and safety boundaries.
5. Review whether the same factual problem affects any of the six language versions.
6. Avoid changing editorial timestamps merely because text was reformatted.

Editors review submissions for evidence, clarity, safety, rights, and consistency. Submission does not guarantee publication or acceptance.

Please observe our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [copyright and licensing guidance](docs/LICENSING.md).
