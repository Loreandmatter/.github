# Lore & Matter — GitHub Organization

Welcome to **Lore & Matter**, a multilingual knowledge project connecting historical skills, natural materials, traditional technologies, and modern evidence-based practices.

Explore the project: **https://loreandmatter.com/**

## About

Lore & Matter studies documented knowledge from the past and examines how it relates to today's science, engineering, crafts, and everyday life. Our articles aim to explain *what people did*, *what the available sources actually establish*, and *when a technique is safe and appropriate today*.

The archive is presented in **six languages**: Serbian, English, German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. It covers building and energy, water systems, agriculture, food preservation, the home, crafts, health and care, and science.
