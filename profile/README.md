# Lore & Matter

### From the lessons of the past. For the world we create.

**[Explore the knowledge archive](https://loreandmatter.com/en/)**

Lore & Matter is a multilingual knowledge project exploring historical technologies, traditional skills, natural materials, and the connections between past experience and modern scientific understanding.

Our purpose is to **preserve useful knowledge, examine the evidence behind it, and explain its relevance for the present**. We value careful historical attribution, practical clarity, and responsible, safety-aware guidance.

### Two perspectives, one archive

**Lore — Old Ways** explores the methods, crafts, inventions, and materials people used throughout history.

**Matter — New Ways** considers modern research, engineering, tools, and adaptations that can help us understand, evaluate, and sometimes improve upon those methods.

### Areas of knowledge

- **Building & Energy** — materials, structures, heating, and energy systems
- **Water & Systems** — collection, storage, treatment, and distribution
- **Agriculture** — soil, cultivation, and ecological practices
- **Food & Preservation** — traditional and contemporary preservation knowledge
- **Home & Everyday Life** — domestic tools, materials, and routines
- **Crafts & Tools** — hand skills, workshops, and historic techniques
- **Health & Care** — historical practices considered alongside modern evidence and safety
- **Science & Discovery** — experiments, discoveries, and the development of ideas

### Knowledge across six languages

The archive is available in **Serbian, English, German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese**. The aim is consistent meaning and evidence across languages, with terminology that reads naturally in each one.

### Our approach

- **Evidence first:** distinguish primary documentation, research findings, tradition, and interpretation.
- **Historical accuracy:** avoid presenting the earliest surviving record as proof of the world's first invention.
- **Safety and context:** explain the limits, conditions, and risks of practical techniques.
- **Transparent improvement:** welcome corrections and stronger sources.
- **Accessible learning:** make complex topics understandable without overstating certainty.

### Contribute to better knowledge

Found an incorrect date, an unsupported claim, a translation issue, or a missing safety qualification? We welcome clear corrections and source suggestions. Include the article URL, language, affected passage, and supporting evidence.
